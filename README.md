# AtmoSync

AtmoSync analyzes local weather differences to help energy analysts identify and evaluate potential market opportunities.

## The problem

Weather can vary significantly between nearby locations, affecting renewable energy generation, local demand, and energy prices. Those differences can be hard to compare across forecasts and market data. AtmoSync aims to bring those signals together in one place.

## Intended first user and market

The initial user is an energy analyst or researcher exploring weather-related opportunities in regional energy markets. The first release will focus on one geographic region and use historical or simulated data for analysis.

## MVP

The first version will:

- Collect weather forecasts for selected locations.
- Compare weather conditions across nearby areas.
- Combine those signals with market data where available.
- Display potential opportunities with the inputs and assumptions behind each estimate.

AtmoSync is an analytics prototype. Its estimates need validation and are not financial advice or automated trading instructions.

## Data sources and limitations

The project may use weather forecasts, weather observations, and energy market data. Each source, its license, update frequency, and geographic coverage should be documented before use.

Data availability and forecast accuracy vary by location and provider. Missing, delayed, or imprecise data can affect the analysis. Early results should be treated as exploratory until tested against historical outcomes.
